A memory care companion

Some connections fade.
We help you hold on to them.

Smriti helps people living with Alzheimer's stay oriented, safe and engaged — and gives the people caring for them one clear place to keep watch, day to day.

How Smriti helps

Memory aids

Daily routines, medication reminders, and a photo memory bank of family and friends, ready to recall on request.

Gentle exercises

Short recall games and word association tasks that adapt in difficulty, designed to keep the mind engaged, not overwhelmed.

Safety & oversight

An emergency contact always one tap away, and a caretaker dashboard that shows routine adherence at a glance.

Face-scan access

No passwords to forget. A quick face scan gets the patient straight into their own dashboard.

                Know About Application

                Everything in Smriti, what it's for, and how to use it.

                • Recognize a Person

                  Dashboard, next to your progress chart

                  Point the camera at someone and tap Scan to see their name, relation to you, and any memory notes. If they're not recognized yet, you can add them right there with a photo. Tap "Search people" to browse everyone you've registered, with real photos.

                • Today's Timetable

                  Dashboard, Schedule section

                  Your daily routine -- meals, medication, bathing, and more. Each task has a start and end time, with sound reminders as it's due. Tap Confirm once you've done it, and it's marked with a tick; if you miss it, it shows a cross. You can add, edit, or delete tasks any time.

                • Important Events

                  Dashboard, beside the Timetable

                  Add one-off appointments, birthdays, or other special dates. You'll get a sound alert on the day and time you chose. Tap OK on the alert once you've seen it, and the event is cleared automatically.

                • Quiz & Games

                  "Quiz & Games" button at the top, or tap your weekly score card

                  A short quiz every day, plus a different mini-game each day, to keep your mind gently active. Questions get a little easier or harder depending on how you're doing. Use the History button inside to see which days you completed, and catch up on any you missed.

                • Find My Things

                  Dashboard, beside Current Location

                  Photograph an item and note where you put it, like "Party coat -- upper shelf of the almirah". Later, tap Search to browse everything you've saved, with real photos, or search by name or location to jog your memory.

                • Current Location

                  Dashboard

                  Shows your real location on a live map, using your device's own GPS. Tap "Refresh live location" any time to update it -- useful for your caretaker to know where you are if needed.

                • Profile

                  "Profile" button at the top

                  Your personal details, your caretaker's contact info, and settings like your neighbour's phone number. Keep this up to date so the Emergency screen always has the right numbers.

                • Emergency SOS

                  Red "SOS" button, always visible in the bottom-right corner

                  For a real emergency. One tap shows Police, Medical, Fire, your Caretaker, and Neighbour numbers, ready to call immediately, plus a way to message your caretaker on WhatsApp.

                • Ask Smriti

                  Violet "Ask Smriti" button, bottom-left corner

                  Type or speak a question -- about the app, your day, or Alzheimer's care generally. It can look up your real tasks, people, and events, and can even add a new task or event for you. Works in English, Hindi, or Hinglish.
